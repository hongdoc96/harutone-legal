HaruTone Android — Privacy policy

Effective date: 2026-11-07

HaruTone is operated by Hongdoc Studio in the Republic of Korea. Business registration: 355-58-00987. For privacy questions, requests or business information, contact contact@hongdocstudio.com.

Camera and on-phone processing

HaruTone uses the front camera, after your notice and permission, to measure colors and let you compare colors beside your face. Camera images, face outlines and landmarks are processed in your phone's memory. Face photos are not saved as files or sent to Hongdoc Studio or another recipient. Images and face geometry are cleared after measurement; one image remains in memory during the color comparison and is cleared when that flow ends, is cancelled, or is interrupted. With Color check, the rear camera (or the front camera on phones without one) reads the color in the center of the live view in memory. No photo is taken, saved or sent, and only a color you choose to save is kept, as a palette color ID.

The app does not use face geometry to identify you. It is a style reference, not a medical service. Your first and second candidate results and their probabilities describe relative closeness to a reference model. Lighting, tanning and hair color can affect them.

Information stored on your phone

Color numbers and results stay on this phone until you delete them in Settings or remove the app. Color-comparison answers are removed after 30 days. Consent records, your language preference and purchase-status cache are also stored locally. This local app data is excluded from cloud backup. We do not receive a copy of these on-phone records.

You do not need an account, and the app does not ask for your name or email address to measure colors. It does not request your location or an advertising identifier. Turning camera measurement off does not send your existing results to us.

Google ML Kit

The app uses Google ML Kit for on-device face detection. Google states that ML Kit processes its input and output on the device. Separately, ML Kit sends API performance and usage metrics to Google and may contact Google for model or compatibility information. Depending on the feature and SDK version, disclosed metrics include device and app information, installation-level identifiers, performance, API settings, events and errors. These metrics are separate from your face images and color results.

Read ML Kit Terms & Privacy, the iOS data disclosure, the Android data disclosure and Google's Privacy Policy. Google handles these metrics under its privacy policy. The app does not provide a separate switch for ML Kit metrics. Turning off camera measurement or deleting local app data does not erase metrics already held by Google.

Free Android edition

All features in this Android edition are free. The app has no ads or in-app purchases and does not start a store payment or purchase-restoration flow. Any purchase-status cache from an earlier version remains only on this phone; access to this edition does not depend on that cache.

Contact messages

If you email support, your email address and the contents you choose to send are processed in the Republic of Korea to answer your request. Do not send face photos, measurement CSVs or sensitive information with a general support question. We delete support correspondence within 90 days after your request is closed. The app's local data deletion does not automatically delete a support email; contact the address above for that request.

Sharing, retention and your choices

If you choose Share or Export, the system share sheet passes a face-free result card, palette wallpaper or PDF to the app or location you choose. It does not include a camera image. Exported copies are managed by your chosen destination; deleting HaruTone local data cannot delete those copies.

Hongdoc Studio does not sell your camera images, face geometry or on-phone color records. The SDK metrics described above have their own processing arrangements. We do not claim that every type of SDK data stays on the phone. Requests involving Google-held metrics are subject to the provider's applicable process and retention. Google may process them in the United States and other countries described in its privacy policy; the app does not set Google's retention period.

You can revoke camera permission through your phone settings, turn camera measurement off in HaruTone, or delete the local measurements, results and settings in the app. If you need help exercising applicable privacy rights, contact us using the email address above.

HaruTone is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. It does not show advertising aimed at children. The app does not ask you to enter your birth date. Store and operating-system age restrictions may also apply.

We will update this policy when practices change. A material change to camera processing will require an updated notice and consent before that processing. The effective version will be available in the app and on its published privacy-policy page. Published privacy policy · Support

カメラと端末内の処理

事前の説明と同意、カメラ権限の許可を得た後、前面カメラで色を測り、顔の横に表示する色を見比べます。カメラ画像、顔の輪郭、ランドマークはスマホのメモリ内で処理します。顔の写真をファイルに保存したり、Hongdoc Studioや第三者に送信したりしません。測定が終わると画像や顔の形の情報をメモリから削除します。見比べ中だけ写真1枚をメモリに残し、終了・中止・中断時に削除します。カラーチェックでは、背面カメラ（ない端末では前面カメラ）の映像の中央の色をメモリ内で読み取ります。写真を撮影・保存・送信することはなく、保存を選んだ色だけをパレットの色IDとして残します。

顔の形の情報を使って個人を識別することはありません。HaruToneはスタイルの参考情報であり、医療サービスではありません。イエベ・ブルベを含む1・2候補と割合は、基準モデルへの近さを表します。光・日焼け・ヘアカラーで結果が変わることがあります。

スマホに保存する情報

色の数値と結果は、設定から削除するか、アプリを削除するまでスマホに保存します。見比べの回答は30日後に削除します。同意記録、言語設定、購入状態のキャッシュも端末内に保存します。これらのアプリ内データはクラウドバックアップの対象外です。当社はそのコピーを受け取りません。

アカウント登録は不要です。色の測定のために氏名やメールアドレスを入力する必要はありません。位置情報や広告識別子も要求しません。カメラ測定をオフにしても、保存済みの結果を当社に送信することはありません。

Google ML Kit

顔の検出には、端末内で動作するGoogle ML Kitを使用します。Googleは、ML Kitの入力と出力を端末内で処理すると説明しています。一方で、APIの性能・利用状況に関する統計はGoogleに送信され、モデルや互換性の情報のためにGoogleへ接続する場合があります。機能やSDKのバージョンによって、端末・アプリ情報、インストール単位の識別子、性能、API設定、イベント、エラーなどが開示されています。これらの統計と、顔の写真や色の結果は別に扱います。

ML Kitの規約とプライバシー、iOS向け開示、Android向け開示、Googleのプライバシーポリシーをご確認ください。Googleは、同社のプライバシーポリシーに従って統計を取り扱います。アプリにはML Kitの統計だけを停止する設定はありません。カメラ測定をオフにしたり端末内データを削除したりしても、Googleがすでに保有する統計は消去されません。

無料のAndroid版

Android版のすべての機能は無料です。広告やアプリ内購入はなく、ストアの決済や購入の復元は行いません。以前のバージョンの購入状態が保存されている場合も、このスマホ内だけに残ります。この版の機能を利用するために購入状態は必要ありません。
