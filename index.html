HaruTone Android support

HaruTone offers on-device personal color measurements, color comparisons, palettes and color checking as a style reference. It is not a medical service or an accuracy guarantee.

All features in the first Android edition are free, with no ads or in-app purchases. App interface languages are English and Japanese.

Privacy and data

Face images stay in memory. Google ML Kit separately sends performance and usage metrics as described in the privacy policy. Delete local records in the app settings. Exported face-free cards, wallpapers and PDFs are managed by the destination you choose.

Contact

Email contact@hongdocstudio.com with your app version, device model and the steps that led to the issue. Do not attach face photos, measurement CSVs or sensitive information to a general support question.
